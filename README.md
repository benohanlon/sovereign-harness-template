# Sovereign Harness template

[Use this template](https://github.com/benohanlon/sovereign-harness-template/generate) to create a repository named `sovereign-harness-<username>`. Select **Private** before adding your information.

In your private copy, start with [your profile](sovereign.md). Record the goals, preferences and context you want your AI to carry between assignments. List your chosen roles and working homes below. Keep credentials outside Git.

| Role or project | Shared role definition and accepted revision | Private working home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Your first role or project] | [Link and revision] | [Where to resume] |

The [OODA Harness](https://github.com/benohanlon/ooda-harness) provides reusable roles, skills and methods. Follow the [setup guide](https://github.com/benohanlon/ooda-harness/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md) to connect your private copy. The [private home guide](https://github.com/benohanlon/ooda-harness/blob/main/docs/private-harness-template.md) explains how to extend it as your team grows.

The starter files use the [MIT licence](LICENSE). Your personal context belongs in your private copy.
